# For agents

This recipe's settings are the Knobs table in README.md and `.env.example`, and `knobs.sh` turns each knob into a gr8 option. The numbers on the card are in `kindling.json`.
Every other gr8 option is in `gr8/KNOBS.md` (the submodule), and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash's boot and measured trade-offs are in `gr8/docs/DEEPSEEK-BOOT.md` and `gr8/docs/FIRST-BOOT.md`.
